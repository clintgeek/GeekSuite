## 1. The verdict

**Can someone use it without instruction? For logging food, yes.** This is the first time the
answer has been yes.
- "What did you eat?" → a sentence → Log it → "✓ Added to lunch" with Fix and Undo all works
  first time.
- The composer, the result card, the Fix sheet and the day question are now the best part of
  the app.

**The risk has moved to the edges around the food box.** Three kinds:
1. **Quantity is invisible.** Home never shows "×2", and "Make it 2" misfires after Heather's
   habitual second tap.
2. **Old screens never got the Simple treatment:** the Food Log rows, Edit Entry, Copy Meal,
   Medications, Settings and the calorie plan.
3. **A few screens tell the user something untrue:** a failed Home load looks like an empty day,
   BP says "Normal" over an elevated week, and the weight card says "faster than goal" and
   "88 days behind" at once.

**Visually, Market Morning works where it was built.** Home, the meal cards, Simple, the
composer, More, Settings, the sidebar and dark mode all hold up. The rest still carries three
older colour systems:
- Studio Slate's teal/emerald gradients and emoji on the Food Log meal sections;
- Tailwind traffic-light colours in BP and Reports;
- MUI's cold greys for tracks, disabled buttons and switches.

Two more visual problems run through the app. Basil green is on every button, so the main action
doesn't stand out. And the produce colours have started meaning macros, warnings and "done" as
well as meals.

## 3. By theme

### 3.1 Learnability (Heather)

Wrong data likely:
- Home failed load (#1).
- Make it 2 (#2).
- Home amounts (#3).
- Copy Meal default (#4).
- A second weight for a day silently replaces the first. The dialog should say "You already have
  218.7 for today. Saving replaces it." (L5)
- In a comma-separated sentence, a day word splits the meal: the chicken lands in yesterday's
  lunch and the fries in dinner (L6).

Blocks a task:
- **"We ate the same dinner" has no path for Heather.** Chef can't send it, and pulling it via
  Copy Meal needs five unlabelled steps. Fix: a "Same as Chef's dinner" pick in the add sheet,
  built on the existing copy path (L7).
- The new-user target wizard (#11).

Slows her down:
- Save placement and no Done (#13).
- Undo all sits beside Undo last, with no redo (L11).
- Full's toast hides the "Just added" strip (L12).
- The Amount control is an unlabelled sliders icon, and Again chips can't set an amount (L14).
- Simple Food Log (#12).
- **Medications is a pharmacist's form:** export first, button-driven search, "RxCUI",
  "indications". It needs a Simple add: "What's it called?" / "When do you take it?" (L16).
- **Simple Settings shows Garmin email/password** and a Units setting that nothing reads (L17).
- Offline, the mic says the microphone is broken, and "your words are saved here" only holds
  while the sheet is open (L18).
- **Simple → Full is one stray tap with no undo** (L19).

Polish:
- "two eggs" isn't detected as a sentence; number words aren't counted (L21).
- Yesterday vs weekday naming mixed in one sheet (L22).
- The meds checklist is today-only (L23).
- "Please sign in again" has nothing to tap (L24).

### 3.2 Visual design

Legibility:
- Meal sections (#7). Track (#10). Names (#8). Status, spinner and focus (#15).
- Weight readings dots at 1.9:1 (A6).
- The start weight greyed to "disabled" (A7).
- Disabled Log it is cold grey every time the sheet opens, so it can read as broken (A8).
- Dark mode: the snackbar sinks below the page, Fix pill borders vanish, and cards barely
  separate (A9, A10).

Consistency:
- **Basil overload** (#9).
- **One colour per job** (B2): produce colours for meals only; a `macroColors` ramp from basil and
  browns; warning/info banners on `warning`/`info`; selected and done in basil. Reports uses a
  third macro palette.
- **Stray Tailwind/MUI hexes**, about 35 in about 12 files: BP stages, the cyan heart glow,
  `#EF4444` delete, the amber star (B3).
- **Retired caps and monospace** in about 15 places, including "MMHG", which is a units error (B4).
- **Shapes:** 5 chip shapes become 2; button radii drift; ThumbFab and the mic aren't pills;
  swollen nested tiles (B5, B6).
- **Delete/edit/close** use three icon treatments each (B7).
- **Dialog anatomy:** primary action sometimes in the header, sometimes the footer (B8).
- **Title Case** (B9). **Heading weights** (B10). **An unstyled MUI Switch** (B11).

Polish:
- FABs cover content (C1).
- The snackbar covers the strip (C2).
- Profile cards bleed off the left edge (C3).
- The calorie plan page sits in an extra paper column, and its stepper clips ("Calori") (C4).
- A tiny ring number on desktop (C5).
- "0days" (C7). Empty Simple tiles stretch (C8).
- Chart tooltips clipped or one word per line (top-10 #6, D-4).
- A deficit shown in error red (C15).

The **contrast test is stale**: `themeContrast.test.js` still checks the sidebar against Studio
Slate teal and never checks the produce pairs. Fix it so the next sweep is guarded.

### 3.3 Navigation, copy and data (mostly Chef)

- **Settings and Profile overlap.** Household appears on both. The name and goal from the first
  run can't be edited anywhere. Settings saves three different ways on one page. Fold Profile
  into Settings, and save everything on change (IA-2, IA-3).
- **Garmin data lives in four places.** Sleep and HRV each appear three times, and two of those
  pages have no harness scene. Proposal: one page with Today / Last night / Trends (IA-4).
- **Many names for the target:** target / goal / plan / Daily Target / Calorie Goal Today. Use
  the glossary (IA-7).
- **Simple's "Everything else"** lists dead ends (Activity, Health dashboard, Import a scan)
  without saying what they need (IA-9).
- **Reports:**
  - the range toggle doesn't control the first section;
  - comparison windows differ card to card;
  - "279 min / week" is labelled as an average;
  - the day ribbon uses ISO dates and bars with no scale;
  - meal rows don't say whether they're totals or per day;
  - the AI panels use four "coach" names and never state their source, time, or that they can be
    wrong (D-5 to D-11).
- **Orphans:** `WeightGoalWizard.jsx` and `GarminSummaryCard.jsx` are imported nowhere (IA-10).

**Copy style sheet.** `ia-copy-data.md` §4 has the full proposal: 13 rules and a glossary of about
25 terms, built from the app's own best lines. The short version:
- Sentence case. "I" when the app thinks for you, "you" otherwise, never "we".
- **Add** food, **Log** a check-in, **Save** an edit, **Remove** + Undo.
- "Couldn't … Try again."
- "cal" with a number, "calories" in a sentence, never kcal. "lb", never lbs.
- Commas at 1,000. "Oct 13". Never ISO.
- No colour without words. Red is for errors only.

## 6. Keep

- **The composer and its voice:**
  - "Tell me what you ate".
  - "Say it like you'd tell a friend. I'll work out the calories."
  - "That didn't go through. Tap Log it to try again."
  - The Fix sheet.
  - "Log this to yesterday? [Yes] [No, today]".
  - The result card, and Undo all restoring the words.
- **Simple Home:** "What did you eat?" above the fold, "You have 1,538 calories left today", the
  plate ring in meal colours, the Today strip, "Did you take your meds?".
- **The Home meal cards:** the identity at its best. Every other meal surface should copy them.
- **Body composition:** "Of the 2.9 lb you've lost, about 2.2 lb was fat and 0.6 lb lean", the
  best sentence in the app.
- **Reports' honesty lines:** "Averages cover the 5 of 7 days you logged.", and the others.
- **The undo grammar, the honest offline banner, the ErrorBoundary copy, and the three-step
  first run.**
- **Market Morning in both modes, the chalkboard sidebar, Nunito with tabular figures, Larger
  text, and the four-tab bar.**
